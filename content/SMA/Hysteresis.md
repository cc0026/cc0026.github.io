## 1. Definition

Thermal hysteresis represents the **irreversibility** of the martensitic transformation. It is the temperature gap between the forward and reverse transformations and reflects energy dissipated in each cycle through internal friction, defect generation, and incompatibility stresses.

## 2. Relationship to Functional Fatigue

In [[Shape Memory Alloy]], thermal hysteresis and fatigue are interconnected through the microstructural irreversibility of the martensitic transformation. During cyclic thermal or thermo-mechanical loading, dislocations can accumulate and residual martensite can be retained. These changes can shift the transformation temperatures and broaden the hysteresis, which is characteristic of functional fatigue.
